# Software projects

Public projects in research workflows, model experimentation, and web development.

## Research workflows

- **[Research Foundry](https://github.com/Xiaoqian-Zhang-Shawno/research-foundry)** — A local workspace for coordinating multiple models, reviewing evidence, and drafting papers.
- **[ShawnoBot](https://github.com/Xiaoqian-Zhang-Shawno/ShawnoBot)** — A Feishu gateway for monitoring a research workstation and running approved tasks.
- **[notion-monthly-research](https://github.com/Xiaoqian-Zhang-Shawno/notion-monthly-research)** — Collects arXiv papers and turns them into a recurring literature digest in Notion.

## Model experiments

- **[distrill-0.6bllm](https://github.com/Xiaoqian-Zhang-Shawno/distrill-0.6bllm)** — An experimental distillation pipeline that trains a compact Qwen model on DeepSeek outputs using QLoRA.

## Independent projects

- **[BetDogEye](https://github.com/Xiaoqian-Zhang-Shawno/BetDogEye)** — A browser-based app for recording sports bets, tracking returns, and visualizing portfolio risk. Records are stored locally, with CSV and JSON export.

## Forks with original changes

- **[CCF Deadlines](https://github.com/Xiaoqian-Zhang-Shawno/ccf-deadlines)** — Extends the [upstream conference tracker](https://github.com/ccfddl/ccf-deadlines) with iCalendar feeds and scheduled publishing, including a feed focused on CCF-A submission deadlines.
